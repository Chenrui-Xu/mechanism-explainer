Choose your agent in the installer. English by default; use another language when you explicitly request it.

  1. 01
    Say what is true.

    Use clear sentences without losing conditions, uncertainty, or the limits of an analogy.

  2. 02
    Show what changes.

    Keep the words beside the picture. Link named parts to the shapes they explain.

  3. 03
    Make them agree.

    Drive the drawing, values, and current explanation from the same model state.

This skill combines STE-inspired writing guidance, Visual Explainer patterns, and the Anatomy SVG core. It starts with the reader's question, then chooses the words and visual form that help answer it.

The examples are simplified educational models with visible limits. The repository includes their source, reusable templates, and tests. Each upstream project retains its license and attribution; this independent project does not imply their endorsement.
