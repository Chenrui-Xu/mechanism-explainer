All three states

  1. The queue has 0 waiting jobs, and the worker is idle. No job has arrived yet.
  2. One job has arrived. The worker has not accepted it yet, so the queue has 1 waiting job.
  3. The worker accepts the job, so the queue has 0 waiting jobs. The job is active; it is not finished yet.

Illustrative sequence: one queue, one worker, one job. It omits retries, failures, timing, and concurrent workers.
